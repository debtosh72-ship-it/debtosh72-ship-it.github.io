01 / Areas of work

Three businesses, connected by operations.

JAGSoM lists Debtosh Chatterjee as CEO and MD at Mrinmoyee Supply and Rishik Commodeal, alongside Chatterjee Cleaning Arts Services. Their own websites show how the group spans cleaning products and workplace support services. The Economic Times company record lists Chatterjee among Cleaning Arts’ directors and gives its incorporation date as 23 July 2004; the page warns its records may not be current.

01 — FACILITY MANAGEMENT

Chatterjee Cleaning Arts

Cleaning Arts says it began in Kolkata in 1992 as a small commercial cleaning and housekeeping business founded by Debtosh. Its current services include integrated facilities management, preventive and office maintenance, payroll, housekeeping, deep cleaning, and specialist carpet, sofa, chair, and floor care. The company lists sectors such as healthcare, education, retail, finance, manufacturing, housing, hospitality, and public services.

Visit Cleaning Arts ↗
02 — CLEANING PRODUCTS

Mrinmoyee Supply

Mrinmoyee describes itself as an Eastern India manufacturer of commercial, industrial, and household cleaning equipment, with bulk orders and pan-India delivery. Its catalogue includes wet and dry mops, wipers, brushes, trolleys, glass-cleaning and high-dusting tools, microfiber cloths, and telescopic poles. LinkedIn company updates also describe in-house moulding, tufting, mop-making, pipe rolling, automated handling, and packing.

03 — WORKPLACE SERVICES

Rishik Commodeal

Rishik Commodeal focuses on payroll processing, temporary staffing, labour and establishment compliance, licenses, audits, and consulting. Its website explains support with remuneration, social contributions, leave, termination procedures, workplace safety, and liaison with government authorities; it lists clients across manufacturing, IT/ITES, telecom, banking, pharma, retail, and hospitality.
